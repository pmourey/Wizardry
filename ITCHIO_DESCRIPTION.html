⚔️ The Ultimate Wizardry Quadrilogy (I, II, III, IV) & The 9 Classic Apple II Scenarios (3D Remaster)

Short description / Tagline:

Faithful 3D & Wireframe remaster of the legendary Apple II Wizardry Quadrilogy (I, II, III, IV: The Return of Werdna) & 9 classic expansions, featuring 13 complete playable scenarios, authentic NTSC color monsters, and original ambient audio.

A faithful modern 3D & Wireframe tribute to the legendary Apple II Dungeon Crawler RPG Quadrilogy, engineered directly from the original UCSD Pascal data structures and binary disk images (`SCENARIO.DATA`).

Une reconstitution moderne et fidèle de la légendaire Quadrilogie Wizardry (I, II, III, IV) et des extensions Apple II, incluant le moteur authentique UCSD Pascal, les musiques d'ambiance et une collection complète de 13 scénarios jouables directement dans votre navigateur.

🏰 13 Complete Playable Scenarios Included / 13 Scénarios Complets Inclus

Switch seamlessly between all 13 complete dungeons (each with its own independent save slot, items, spells, monsters, and maps):

👑 The Official Classic Quadrilogy (Sir-Tech)

  • #1 — Proving Grounds of the Mad Overlord (1981): Defeat the evil archmage Werdna on Floor 10 and recover the sacred Amulet for Overlord Trebor. (10 floors, 101 monsters, 101 items).
  • #2 — Wizardry II: The Knight of Diamonds (1982): Restore the sacred city of Llylgamyn by retrieving the 5 pieces of the legendary Diamond Armor and the Staff of Gnilda. (10 floors, 78 monsters, 130 items, starter party level 9+).
  • #3 — Wizardry III: The Legacy of Llylgamyn (1983): Natural cataclysms threaten the kingdom! Ascend the 6 vertical mountain tiers to reclaim the sacred Orb protected by the celestial dragon L'Kbreth. (6 floors, 78 monsters, 104 items, alignment-restricted sanctums).
  • #4 — Wizardry IV: The Return of Werdna (1987): Turn the tables and play as the evil archmage Werdna himself! Awaken in your bottom-floor tomb, summon monstrous fiends on mystic pentagrams, and battle your way up through Trebor's pyramid past roaming do-gooder adventurers. (10 floors, 101 monsters, inverted pyramid layout).

📜 The 9 Retro Apple II Community Expansions

  • #5 — The Black Stone (Steve Evans, 1985): 8 intense floors with witty riddles, traps, and unique items.
  • #6 — The Catacombs of Vlad (1984): 10 levels of gothic crypts, vampires, and undead fiends.
  • #7 — DragonQuest (Dan Gartung, 1984): High-fantasy expedition featuring 8 dungeon tiers.
  • #8 — Knight of the Grave (1987): Defeat necromancer warlords in a labyrinth of tombs and crypts.
  • #9 — Scarlet Brotherhood of Hsi-So (1985): Infiltrate the fortress of a deadly monastic order of assassins and mages.
  • #10 — Nihonbashi (1986): Oriental-themed campaign with samurai masters, ronins, and ancient shrines.
  • #11 — O'Connor's Mine (1985): Dangerous mineral caverns crawling with elemental horrors and dwarf spirits.
  • #12 — The Emperor's Seal (1986): Recover the lost imperial crest through intricate multi-level traps.
  • #13 — Atomic Wasteland 2098 (Dan Gartung, 1987): Total sci-fi conversion with irradiated bunkers, cyber-mutants, and laser technology.

✨ Key Features / Fonctionnalités Clés

  • Dual 3D Rendering (Three.js): Switch instantly between Textured 3D Stone Corridors with dynamic torch lighting and classic 1981 Apple II Green/White Wireframe mode.
  • Authentic NTSC Color Monster Sprites: Full collection of authentic color Apple II monster bitmaps (including the 30 specific portraits for Wizardry II and Wizardry III).
  • Authentic UCSD Pascal Mechanics:

- 8 Classes: Fighter, Mage, Priest, Thief, Bishop (identifies unknown & cursed items), Samurai, Lord, and Ninja.

- 5 Races & 3 Alignments: Human, Elf, Dwarf, Gnome, Hobbit (Good, Neutral, Evil).

- 50 Iconic Spells: 21 Mage spells (HALITO, MAHALITO, TILTOWAIT, DUMAPIC, MALOR...) and 29 Priest spells (DIOS, MADI, KADORTO, LOKTOFEIT...).

- True 1981 Mortality & Corpse Recovery: Dead heroes leave their remains at their exact `(X, Y, Floor)` coordinates in the maze—send a rescue expedition to bring them back to the Temple of Cant!

- Trapped Chests: Inspect and disarm Poison Needles, Gas Bombs, Crossbolts, Exploding Boxes, Stunners, Teleporters, and Alarm traps.

  • Original Ambient Audio & Fanfares: Atmospheric background dungeon tracks and retro fanfares.
  • Interactive Tactical Mini-Map: Live `7×7` radar and full `20×20` floor cartography with stairs, elevators, pits, chutes, spinners, dark zones, and teleporters.
