### 🔮 1. Interactive In-Game Oracle & Scenario Strategy Guides

Dungeon crawling in the golden age of 1981 was notoriously unforgiving. To empower modern adventurers without sacrificing the mystery and thrills of authentic exploration, we are proud to introduce the **Interactive Oracle & Floor Strategy Guide**!

Accessible at any time via the top header (**Manuals & Guides** / **Guide**), this dynamic briefing system adapts to your personal playstyle with four customizable guidance levels:

- **🚫 Purist Retro Mode (OFF):** For veteran purists craving the unassisted 1981 Apple II experience. No hints, no prophecies—rely purely on your wits, pencil, and graph paper.
- **👁️ Subtle Whispers (SUBTLE):** Atmospheric omens and cryptic clues. Informs you of immediate environmental dangers and floor themes while keeping map discovery 100% untouched.
- **🛡️ Tactical Briefing (STRATEGY - Default):** Comprehensive survival advice drawn from original campaign manuals. Highlights primary floor objectives, spell recommendations (e.g. *Montino* for silencing spellcasters, *Zilwan/Dispel* against undead), key hazards (chutes, spinners, anti-magic fields), and monster counter-strategies (warning against permanent Level-Drain from Vampires and Wraiths).
- **✨ Exhaustive Matrix (EXHAUSTIVE):** Unveils exact `(X, Y)` coordinates for elevators, chutes, key encounters, and staircase connections for players seeking complete cartographic mastery or debugging scenario runs.

*Note on scenario design:* The guides accurately reflect canonical floor topologies—including all **10 floors** of *Proving Grounds of the Mad Overlord* (featuring Werdna's lair at `(0, 0)` and crucial *MALOR* evacuation warnings) and the **6 mountain terraces** of *Wizardry III: Legacy of Llylgamyn*.

### 🎹 2. Original Neoclassical Piano Soundtrack (by Léo Jean)

Exploration in the cold stone corridors of the labyrinth just became vastly more immersive. Thanks to a musical collaboration with fellow developer and pianist **Léo Jean** ([@LeoJean890 on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@LeoJean890)), we have integrated a suite of 10 original piano compositions directly into the dungeon exploration playlist!

- **Atmospheric Rotation:** Léo Jean's poignant piano pieces (*Balades au fil de l'eau*, *Mélancolie*, *Crépuscule*, *Errance*, *Rêveries*...) play dynamically alongside the iconic retro dungeon themes, enhancing tension, solitude, and wonder as you delve deeper.
- **Sleek Audio Screener Widget:** A discreet music overlay in the lower left corner displays the current track name, evocative mood description, and a direct link to support the artist on YouTube.
- **Broadcaster Protection:** Clear licensing terms are built right into the music player tooltip, ensuring streamers and content creators can broadcast gameplay safely with proper attribution.

### 📱 3. Responsive Mobile Fullscreen Mini-Map & UI Polish

Following feedback from players exploring on smartphones, tablets, and handhelds, the tactical interface received several vital ergonomic updates:

- **True Mobile Fullscreen Cartography:** The full tactical mini-map modal now dynamically stretches across high-DPI smartphone and tablet screens without awkward margins or letterboxing.
- **Cleaned Control Overlays:** Eliminated redundant minimap buttons and unified spell buff status indicators (*Milwa*, *Lomilwa*, *Map Active*) for a clutter-free view.
- **Complete Bilingual Localization:** All interface components, cheat menus, spell cards, and scenario guides are now fully available in both **English** and **French** with instant on-the-fly toggling.
- **Defensive Runtime Hardening:** Fixed array safety checks across floor rendering components to ensure smooth transitions on deep dungeon floors (Floors 6 through 10).
